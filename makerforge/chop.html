3 · Cut
Drag the handles in the viewport to place the cut plane.
More than 1 lays down that many evenly-spaced parallel planes through the whole piece (shown as a preview below), producing planes+1 pieces in one Commit Cut. Up to 256 planes.
Splits off everything on the near side of the current plane as one unsplit piece first, so a low-lying disconnected feature (e.g. a tail near the paws) doesn't land in the first slice.
Flexi Cut
Repositions just this one plane (shown in amber below) along the full length of the piece; the rest stay evenly spaced.
Lays down evenly-spaced planes across X, Y and Z together, like a 3D waffle — (X+1) × (Y+1) × (Z+1) pieces in one Commit Cut.
Offsets the Y cut every other X row, like a brick course, so seams don't run straight through in a line — needs both X and Y divisions set. Not available together with Adjust Grid Plane overrides.
Adjust Grid Plane
Repositions just this one division plane (shown brighter below) along its axis — the model stays exactly where it loaded, and the rest of that axis's planes stay evenly spaced.
Direction (rotation axis)
Cuts like a pie, through a shared axis — evenly spaced wedges around Direction, starting at Start angle. Center U/V shifts the rotation axis off the piece's own center (0 = centered, ±1 = piece edge) in the two axes perpendicular to Direction.
Repeatedly splits the selected piece along its oversized axis until every part fits the bed above.
